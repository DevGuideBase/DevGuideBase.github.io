Common data processing and reporting mistakes mistakes in New Projects

Before you implement anything above, make sure you are not stepping on one of these well-known rakes:
  • Treating output as text — parse objects, never regex your own stdout.
  • Running scripts downloaded from the internet with unrestricted policy.
  • Using aliases (%, ?, gci) inside shared automation scripts.
  • Swallowing errors with -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue in pipelines.
  • Hard-coding credentials instead of SecretManagement vaults.
None of these are exotic. They are the quiet defaults that look fine in review and fail at 3 a.m. Closing even two of them puts your data processing and reporting mistakes setup ahead of most teams we audit.
